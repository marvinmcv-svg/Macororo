TWO-DAY PROJECT: GLOBALIZATION POSTER

Task: Design a poster advertising/promoting one key aspect of globalization
(e.g., Cultural, Technological, Economic, or Political globalization).
Grade Level: Middle / High School Social Studies   |   Total Points: 25   |   Duration: 2 Days

PROJECT OVERVIEW & TIMELINE

Day 1: Research & Concept (Planning & Draft)

  • Select one dimension: Cultural (food, music, language), Technological (internet, global communication, transit), Economic (trade, global brands, supply chains), or Political (international cooperation, global organizations).
  • Research key facts, examples, benefits, and global impact.
  • Draft slogan, visual layout, and key takeaways on a planning sketch.

Day 2: Poster Creation & Polish (Execution)

  • Produce the final poster using clear, compelling visuals and concise messaging.
  • Include a catchy slogan, 3+ factual globalization examples, and an explanation of global connection.
  • Final review against rubric and submission.

POSTER REQUIREMENTS

  • Focus: One specific aspect of globalization clearly identified.
  • Visuals: Relevant, high-impact imagery, symbols, or map elements.
  • Slogan & Messaging: Catchy, informative headline/slogan promoting your chosen aspect.
  • Evidence & Facts: At least 3 specific real-world examples showing interconnectedness.
  • Reflection/Call to Action: Clear explanation of how this connects people across borders.
